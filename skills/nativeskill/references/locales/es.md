# Spanish (`es`)

Left to right. Cardinal plurals are `one` and `other`.

## Voice

Pick `tú` or `usted` and keep it. Spain (`es-ES`) and Latin America differ: `vosotros` is Spain, `ordenador` versus `computadora`, and the sign-up verb you choose has to match the region the app ships. If the file is generic `es`, do not invent a country. Say which variety you used.

Telegram ships `Registrarse` for sign up and `Cambiar contraseña` for change password. Android ships `Permitir` for Allow, `Denegar` for Deny, and `Saltar` for Skip. `Cancelar` is the dialog word. Do not put it on `billing.stop_renewal`.

## Punctuation

Spanish opens questions and exclamations with `¿` and `¡` when the sentence needs them. Buttons usually have neither. Do not add an exclamation to an error to make it feel urgent.

## Format

`1.234,56` is typical in Spain. Mexico and other locales differ. Use the formatter for the tag you were given (`es-MX`, `es-ES`), not a single pattern for every Spanish file.
