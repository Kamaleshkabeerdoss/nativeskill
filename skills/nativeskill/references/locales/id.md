# Indonesian (`id`)

Latin script, left to right. Cardinal plurals are `other` only. Android's legacy folder is `values-in`. A project that already uses `values-id` should stay on `values-id`. Do not add both for one language.

## Not Malay

Indonesian and Malay share some words and differ on others. devise ships `Mendaftar` for sign up here and `Daftar` for Malay. Do not copy a Malay catalog into `id` or the reverse. Android Indonesian Allow is `Izinkan`, Deny is `Tolak`, and Skip is `Lewati`.

## Voice

Buttons are short imperatives or nouns. No English title case. There is no source-attested Indonesian stop-renewal in the pinned catalogs. A `billing.stop_renewal` proposal is a draft and must say that renewal stops while access continues until the date the product states.

## Format

Use Western digits. Group thousands with the `id-ID` formatter. Rupiah amounts come from the currency formatter when the charge is in IDR. Do not write `Rp` by hand in one string and let the formatter do it in the next.
