# Data sources and notices

Locale metadata is derived from [Unicode CLDR 49](https://cldr.unicode.org/downloads/cldr-49), distributed under the bundled [Unicode License](LICENSE-UNICODE.txt). The registry records the pinned inputs for scripts and plurals. Supplemental regional profiles use CLDR's likely subtags or explicit script/region combinations. They are identified separately from locale tags with CLDR locale data. A private-use region such as CLDR's XK is flagged as such.

IANA, W3C, dictionaries, and terminology sites are linked as references. Their entries and definitions are not bundled. Kaikki is derived from Wiktionary and is used as a descriptive lookup reference. A scoped dictionary, such as White Hmong for `mww`, makes no wording claim for other variants.

The UI concepts are original NativeSkill content. A Foundation pack provides a locale profile and reference links. Native-reviewed wording requires evidence tied to the locale, concept, and usage.

- Telegram for Android strings that are included, GPL-2.0-only ([notice](licenses/telegram.txt)). Those labels stay under GPL-2.0-only. They are not relicensed as MIT.

The dictionary sites cited by the language registry remain lookup links. NativeSkill drafts are original suggestions and reference links do not imply approval of the exact phrase.
